Surrogate Model
Framework

Computational Mechanics — Design Space Exploration

An ensemble framework replacing computationally expensive FEA simulations with high-fidelity surrogate models for mechanical design optimization — enabling real-time design exploration and Pareto optimization for part-specific parametric spaces.

Stack: Python — PyTorch — Scikit-Learn — ANSYS Workbench Domain: Computational Mechanics — Surrogate Modeling — Multi-Objective Optimization
01 — Motivation

Why Surrogate Modeling?

A single high-fidelity FEA simulation of the macro-scale accelerometer takes ~45 minutes on a 32-core workstation. Design space exploration requires thousands of evaluations. Brute-force optimization is intractable.

500 High-fidelity FEA samples (Latin Hypercube DoE)
1.35M× Speedup: ~2 ms inference vs 45 min simulation
6D Parametric space mapped to 6 output objectives
02 — Parametric Space

Six Geometric Parameters

Six geometric parameters define the design space. Outputs: 4 modal frequencies, max deformation, max von Mises stress — 6 objectives total.

03 — Sensitivity

Jacobian Sensitivity Analysis

The Jacobian ∇f(x) reveals how each output responds to parameter perturbations. Global sensitivity heatmaps identify dominant parameters per output, guiding dimensionality reduction and manufacturing tolerance allocation.

Design Manifold Visualization

2D and 3D manifold projections reveal the underlying structure of the design space. The interactive visualizations show how objectives trade off across the parametric domain.

05 — PCA & Correlation

Correlation Structure & Dimensionality

PCA reveals that the first 3 principal components capture 94% of variance in the 6D output space. Strong coupling exists between modal frequencies, while stress and deformation exhibit distinct response patterns.

06 — Pareto Front

Multi-Objective Optimization

Multi-objective optimization (NSGA-II) on the surrogate ensemble yields the Pareto front. The trade-off between frequency separation (modal isolation) and stress/deformation is the key design tension.

07 — Ensemble Performance

Model Benchmarking

The weighted ensemble consistently outperforms individual models across all outputs by leveraging model diversity — Kriging excels at interpolation, RBF at smooth extrapolation, and the MLP ensemble captures nonlinear interactions.

08 — Impact

From Hours to Milliseconds

  • Design Cycle: Reduced from weeks (manual FEA iterations) to hours (surrogate optimization + targeted verification).
  • Insight: Global sensitivity maps directly informed manufacturing tolerance budgets — 40% cost reduction on non-critical dimensions.
  • Validation: 47 Pareto-optimal designs identified; top 3 validated with full FEA — max error 2.3% on critical outputs.
